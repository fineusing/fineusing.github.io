## The Hidden Trap: Apple's Strict Hardware Exclusions

Many Mac users assume that running modern macOS is enough to use AirPlay to Mac. However, Apple locked down native AirPlay Receiver capabilities to a very narrow list of hardware released in 2018 or later:

- MacBook Pro (2018 and later)
- MacBook Air (2018 and later)

## Common Reasons AirPlay Drops or Fails to Connect

Even on officially supported hardware, users constantly encounter frustrating connection failures:
1. **Corporate Network & Wi-Fi Isolation**: Enterprise and campus Wi-Fi networks block mDNS / Bonjour broadcast packets, making it impossible for your iPhone to discover your Mac wirelessly.
2. **Bluetooth Handshake Failures**: Apple's peer-to-peer discovery relies heavily on Bluetooth Low Energy handshakes that frequently desynchronize.
3. **Firewall & VPN Interference**: Work VPNs often redirect local subnet routing, severing the direct AirPlay link between devices.
