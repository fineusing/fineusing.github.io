## The Flaw: Why Apple's Touch Dragging Fails on a Desktop

1. **Tiny Collision Target Zones**: The trigger area to create a folder is far too small. A quick cursor flick is interpreted as an "insert between" command rather than a "create group" command, sending icons scrambling across your screen;
2. **Unpredictable Edge Paging**: Hovering near screen edges often triggers no response, followed by violently flipping three pages past your intended target;
3. **Neglecting the Right Mouse Button**: On a desktop operating system, ignoring the context menu is an architectural crime that wastes valuable user time.
