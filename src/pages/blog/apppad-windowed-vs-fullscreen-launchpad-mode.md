## The Hidden Cost of Full-Screen Interruption

Full-screen application grids were initially conceived for 11-inch and 13-inch MacBooks, where dedicated full-bleed interfaces made sense.

In professional multi-window desktop environments, however, full-screen takeover introduces significant cognitive friction:

1. **Context Switching & Memory Disruption**: Cognitive research in human-computer interaction shows that abruptly blocking out active visual references breaks short-term working memory. When you return from launching an app, your eyes must spend precious seconds relocating your place in your code or manuscript;
2. **Excessive Physical Mouse Travel**: On 21:9 or 32:9 ultrawide screens, moving from the center of your screen to an edge icon requires dozens of inches of physical wrist displacement;
3. **Multi-Display Glitches**: In legacy macOS, summoning full-screen Launchpad on one display frequently caused stutter or focus flickering across connected secondary monitors.
