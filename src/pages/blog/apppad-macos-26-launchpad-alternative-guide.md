## Why Text-Based Search Cannot Replace a Visual Launcher

Searching via Spotlight or Raycast is fast only when you know the exact name of the binary.

In practice, that assumption fails all the time:
1. **Fuzzy Name Recall**: You installed an open-source video compression tool or audio plugin three weeks ago. You remember its icon was purple and the name started with "V" or "Hand", but can't recall the exact spelling. If Spotlight returns zero results, you are forced to open Finder and scroll through hundreds of `.app` bundles manually;
2. **Dock Crowding**: Once you pin more than 20 applications, the macOS Dock shrinks into a strip of unidentifiable micro-icons. Mousing over them is slow, imprecise, and visually cluttered;
3. **Spatial Memory Is Innate**: Human cognition relies heavily on visual landmark memory ("my creative tools live on page two in the top-right group", "my development terminals are organized in the Dev folder").

A dedicated, customizable visual grid remains the most reliable visual control center on a desktop operating system.
