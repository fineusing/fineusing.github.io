DeviceMirror makes iPhone and iPad screens feel at home on macOS. Whether you are presenting a demo, watching a video, or recording a tutorial, the workflow is designed to be simple and stable.

## Wireless mirroring, no extra app
With AirPlay 2 support, you can mirror over Wi‑Fi without installing anything on iOS. It is the fastest way to get a phone screen on your Mac.

## Wired mode for ultra‑smooth output
Need consistent 60 FPS? Plug in with USB for stable, low‑latency mirroring. This is ideal for live demos, gameplay, and screen recording.

## Record what matters
Capture your phone screen and audio as MP4, or save Mac system audio as MP3. It is a clean way to create tutorials and share feedback.

## Built for everyday workflows
DeviceMirror is not just a viewer. It is a utility that fits into the way you already work — presentations, support, content creation, and simple “show it on a bigger screen” moments.

If you want your phone screen to feel like a first‑class desktop experience, DeviceMirror is built for exactly that.
